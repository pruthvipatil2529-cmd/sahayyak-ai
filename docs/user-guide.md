# Sahayyak AI — Elder-Friendly User Guide

Welcome to **Sahayyak AI**, your patient digital assistant!

---

## 1. How to Change Language & Make Text Bigger
At the very top of your screen, you will see the black bar:
* **To change language**: Click on **English**, **हिंदी**, or **मराठी**. The entire app changes immediately!
* **To make text bigger**: Click **A+** (Large) or **A++** (Extra Large) to read comfortably without glasses.
* **To change colors**: Click the sun ☀️ for daytime or the sparkle 🌓 for High Contrast mode.

---

## 2. How to Ask a Question Using Your Voice
1. Click the large green button **Ask Sahayyak AI** or the microphone icon.
2. Tap the round microphone button.
3. Speak your question naturally in English, Hindi, or Marathi (for example: *"How can I send a photo to my daughter on WhatsApp?"*).
4. Tap the button again or pause speaking.
5. Sahayyak AI will write down simple numbered steps.
6. Click **Listen Aloud 🔊** to hear the instructions read out to you!

---

## 3. How to Save a Guide for Later
1. Go to **Browse Guides**.
2. When you find a guide you like (e.g. *Connecting to Wi-Fi*), click the small bookmark icon **Save Guide**.
3. Next time you visit, go to **My Dashboard** to find your saved guides instantly.

---

## 4. Important Safety Rules
* **Never enter your UPI PIN to receive money.**
* **Never read out SMS OTP codes to telephone callers.**
* **If money is deducted by fraud, immediately call 1930 (National Cyber Crime Helpline).**
